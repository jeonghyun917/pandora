## Decisions and non-goals

Reject substring/numeric-set equality, guessed AND/OR, source-specific mappings,
filename-derived legal effect, OCR/security-policy bypass and readiness relaxation.
Only contiguous signed decimal tokens with explicit `%` or `년` and 이상/초과/
이하/미만 are recognized. Keep source cells/score and independent tracks. Require
each track's intervals to cover the line exactly once (no gap/overlap); no logical
rule between tracks or project/actor applicability is inferred.
Context is exact owning parent cell plus cells to its left whose row spans cover
that cell's source row; never pull adjacent-row text or inherited nested values.
Unknown syntax/units/ownership fail closed. Existing serialized evidence cap stays.

PDF hash-only inspection is not geometry/equivalence proof. Any additional native
position protocol must retain resource/environment/deadline/byte-identity guards,
be bounded before stdout, reject malformed/non-finite/over-bound data, and never
claim full rendering equivalence. Legal attachment role requires official direct
evidence and full content correspondence, not broadening the declared field role.
