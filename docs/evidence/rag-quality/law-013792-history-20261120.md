# General effective-history evidence — 2026-11-20

Source: https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsHstListR.do?lsiSeq=283735&lsId=013792&ancYnChk=0

The URL was obtained from the official law page's public history-menu function,
not guessed from a different law. The fetched HTML is preserved next to this
note. It contains four Korean history rows and separate English translation rows.

The two newest Korean rows share edition 283735, Act 21392 and promulgation
2026-02-27. They have different effective-phase dates: 2026-08-28 and 2027-02-28.
Thus 2026-11-18 falls in the earlier phase of this official history response.
This is not a claim that every provision is effective on that date. The delayed
Article 32 and the supplementary commencement clause still require their own
provision-bound proof.

The offline parser binds the source URL's law/edition parameters, exact
promulgation and law number, a complete HTML history response, distinct valid
phase dates, an observed-at date and a reference date inside a closed interval.
It rejects ambiguous parameters, malformed or duplicate dates, truncated HTML,
an open last interval, future observation, a different edition, and requests
for all-provision or specific-article status. The CURRENT display marker is not
used as historical proof.
